A desk is a paper-trading account that replays history one trading day at a time. Each morning the agents (Planner, Bull and Bear analysts, Forecaster, Critic and Exit Monitor) plan trades with data up to the previous close; the session then fills those plans at the open, with Indian delivery costs.
